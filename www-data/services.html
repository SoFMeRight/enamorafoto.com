Our Services

Below are the various packages that we provide.

Pre-wedding

Have a chance to meet the photographer(s) and get a few pictures taken prior to the wedding. Practice the wedding experience to help avoid common mistakes during the event.

Pre-ceremony

Pictures of the bride and groom, and the wedding guests, in their wedding-attire before the ceremony begins.

The Ceremony

The main event: the walk, the vows, the rings, and the kiss.

Reception

Photos of the celebration after the ceremony.

Portrait pictures

Pictures of the entire family, pairings with the bride and groom, and fun photo booth style pictures.

Printing and delivery services

All customers will receive their original and edited photos via USB or cloud storage. You can also purchase PhotoBooks ; Postcards / Wallet photos ; CD / USB ; Prints sizes 8 x 10 / 12 x 18 for additional fees.
