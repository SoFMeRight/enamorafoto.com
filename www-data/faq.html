Frequently Asked Questions

For the curious couples.

What questions should I ask the studio? I have no idea what I’m doing?

No worries, you have landed on the right page, Our FAQ list will help guide you through the process. Please review over the questions we have provided.

What are the advantages of a photography team versus a solo photographer? Are your rates more because you’re not solo?

Photo studios with more than one photographer aren’t uncommon, in fact solo wedding photography with appropriate coverage and lighting is simply impossible. Working as a team helps expedite certain setup processes (such as lighting equipment, depending on the package you choose) and allows for multiple perspectives and coverage of the entire event. Services and rates are based off packages we provide, we offer smaller packages which result in an overall cost reduction.

What types of packages do you offer?

We offer 5 different types of packages. For a list of packages and add-ons, check out our services page. The packages range from our Economy (starting at $500); which includes coverage of the main ceremony and 35 of your favorite moments from your wedding, to our ProPix Package (starting $5500); which includes full day coverage of the main ceremony, pre/post wedding, and the reception. Each package has the option for add-ons (extra poses, more shooting time, number of photographers, etc.) Consult with us via email or in-person and we will help you decide what is right for your wedding.

I’m on a budget, I know wedding photography is expensive, do you have a cheap package for small weddings or minimal coverage?

Yes we do! The Economy Package is our most affordable option, starting at $500. This includes the main ceremony and 35 of your favorite moments from your wedding.

What’s availability look like? How far in advance do I need to book?

At least two weeks out. We have numerous projects that our studio works on at any given time, so the earlier you book the better. Also consider test shoots and preliminary samples.
